01 / ABOUT

From the helpdesk
to the data center floor.

Three degrees, two careers, one direction: networking infrastructure.

I started in physical security working shifts as an officer and supervisor at Allied Universal and Securitas. That gave me a ground-level understanding of access control, incident documentation, and risk that most IT people never get. From there I moved into desktop support at Franklin University, troubleshooting hardware, software, and network issues for staff and students every day.

I passed CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) in April 2026, satisfying DoD 8570 IAT Level II requirements, and followed it with the ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) in May 2026. I'm now pursuing CCNA certification to build deeper networking expertise — routing, switching, and the protocols that keep infrastructure running.

Since July 2026 I've been working as a Data Center Technician at a hyperscale AI data center, installing and maintaining low-voltage and fiber cabling infrastructure, performing rack-and-stack builds, and troubleshooting port-level connectivity for critical systems. I'm building on that hands-on infrastructure experience, and my CCNA studies, to grow into Network Technician, NOC, and Data Center Operations roles.
